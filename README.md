## Provenance and coding

Study IDs P1–P65 follow the author-confirmed extraction sheet fulltext (2) in Jul23-UnitOfAnalysis CG.xlsx. P12 is linked to manuscript reference [55]; P37 is correctly identified as the accounting-ethics paper at reference [94]. Original extraction notes are preserved. The study register omits abstracts and internal reviewer flags.

E means an extraction note is recorded; U means no extraction note was supplied. U is not evidence of absence. E does not establish direct empirical support. Risk Outcomes entries marked E were retrospectively classified from explicit adverse-consequence notes already present in other source columns. This does not establish complete Risk Outcomes coverage. Governance measures and adoption barriers were not automatically treated as observed harms.

The matrix is an extraction-coverage audit, not independently verified D/I/N coding. Supplied statements, numerical claims and page references have not been independently rechecked against full texts in preparing this supplement. P37 page references (998–1043) differ from its journal pagination (401–414). The manuscript's direct/indirect evidence markers require separate verification.

Publication years in the register are 2022: 3; 2023: 4; 2024: 9; 2025: 14; 2026: 35. The 2026 share is 53.8% (54% rounded), consistent with the manuscript. P18 is the service-industry paper, not the similarly titled banking-industry paper.

Only the Scopus strategy was supplied. The manuscript reports early July 2026, but no exact execution date was supplied. This query was not rerun. Other database strategies, independent coder ratings and report-level exclusion records are not included. No inter-coder agreement coefficients or numerical quality-appraisal ratings were created retrospectively.

## Search restrictions

TITLE requires an AI term and at least one adoption, appropriation, implementation, readiness or governance term. Three further mandatory TITLE-ABS-KEY conditions constrain responsibility proximity, organizational context, and mechanisms. These restrictions may underretrieve post-adoption work. DOCTYPE ar and re retrieve articles and reviews; retrieval does not establish final eligibility. No supplementary search or snowballing was performed during preparation of this supplement.
